## 8. What this report does not close

* **Population.** The gate watches theorems that both extractors cover: `MachLib.*` theorems whose names pass `extract_statement.lean`'s substring filter. `Certcom.*` theorems (in the statement census, not in `extract.lean`'s scope) and hand-written theorems whose names contain `.rec`, `.inj`, `.below`, `.eq_`, `proof_` (e.g. `recip_ge`, `injective_of_antitone`) are not watched: 301 `Certcom.*` theorems (from the evidence), and roughly 70 hand-written `MachLib.*` ones (a name-based estimate made on 2026-10-13 from `artifacts/machsig_raw.tsv`, out of 1,462 names the filter excludes, most of them genuinely generated). Widening the population is an extractor change, and so its own reviewed transition; it is not done here.
* **Cost.** `extract.lean` takes 8.5-10.7 min here; `#print axioms` over every watched theorem takes seconds, so the time is not the axiom walk (the c3046961 note blames the unshared `constsIn` walk).
* **Review.** Removals, restatements and every change to a proof assumption are in the packet for a person to judge. Nothing here accepts them.
