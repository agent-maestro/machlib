## W1. The rule

A theorem is watched when its module is `MachLib`, `MachLib.*` or `CertcomLibm`, or its name is under `MachLib` or `Certcom`, and tools/theorem_ledger's exclusion rule does not exclude it. Private theorems (`_private.<module>.0.<name>`) are decided by `isPrivateName` and judged by their user name, never by spelling. The rule is the theorem ledger's, copied into extract_trust.lean and extract_statement.lean, so the gate watches exactly what MachLib counts as its theorems (the ledger's published total is 9,431).
